About Ciauru

The name “Ciauru” comes from the Sicilian word meaning “aroma”—a memory of the irresistible and comforting smell that fills Italian homes, where you can taste the love with every bite.

Here at Ciauru, we aim to share the experience of genuine Italian flavour with you through Sicilian hospitality.

From dining in at our relaxed restaurant to ordering home for a cosy night in, our goal is to share our homemade quality with every costumer.
